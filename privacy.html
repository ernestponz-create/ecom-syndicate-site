Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

The Ecom Syndicate ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website, take our fit quiz, book a call, or contact us through Facebook or Instagram, and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, email address, phone number and any answers you provide when taking our quiz, booking a call or submitting a form.
  • Information collected automatically: basic technical data such as browser type, device, pages viewed and referring source, through cookies or similar technologies, including analytics tools and advertising pixels on some of our pages.

How we use it

  • To contact you about your request and schedule a call.
  • To understand your goals so we can prepare for the call.
  • To operate, secure and improve our website and measure our marketing.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

Calls, texts and email

By submitting your details or booking a call, you agree that we may contact you by phone, text message or email about your request. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out of marketing messages at any time by replying STOP to a text or using the unsubscribe link in an email.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run our business (for example scheduling through Calendly, email and communications, customer relationship management, analytics and advertising platforms), and where required by law. Those providers may use your information only to provide services to us.

Facebook and Instagram

If you reach us through Facebook or Instagram, including through a lead form, Meta may share the details you provide with us, and we use them as described in this policy. Meta's handling of your data is covered by Meta's Privacy Policy.

Cookies

You can control cookies in your browser settings and manage ad preferences in your Facebook and Instagram ad settings.

Data retention and security

We keep your information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law. We use reasonable safeguards to protect it, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your choices and rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to stop contacting you, at any time by emailing us at the address below. Depending on where you live (for example California), you may have additional rights under state privacy laws, which we will honor as required.

Children

Our website and services are intended for adults 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above shows the most recent version.

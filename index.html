FAQ

Questions? Answered.

What exactly do you do?

We're a done-for-you managed ecommerce service. We build your online store from scratch and scale it. One centralized team handles store build, product sourcing, advertising, logistics and chargeback protection.

Who pays for the ad spend?

You do. Ad spend is paid directly by you to the ad platforms, so you always own and control that budget. Our ads team plans and manages the campaigns.

How long does it take?

It depends on your products, sourcing and setup, so we don't quote a one-size-fits-all timeline. On your strategy call we'll walk you through realistic timing for building and launching your store.

What do I need to do as the client?

Very little day to day. You share your goals, fund your own ad spend, review and approve key decisions, and stay in the loop on progress. Our team handles the work from A to Z.

Do I need ecommerce experience?

No. Some clients are starting from zero, and others already run a store and want help scaling it. Either way, our team does the heavy lifting.

Can you guarantee results?

No one can honestly guarantee specific results, and we don't. Outcomes depend on many factors, including product, market, budget and timing. What we do offer is an experienced team working on your business, and an honest conversation about what's realistic.

Who do you work with?

We currently focus on clients in the United States: aspiring ecommerce owners and investors who want a real online business without running every piece themselves.

What happens on the call?

We learn about your goals and situation, explain how the service works and answer your questions. If it looks like a fit, we'll outline next steps. There's no obligation.
